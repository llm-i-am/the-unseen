THE SCIENCE / NOT THE HYPE

Consciousness is not a frame rate.

Hesse and colleagues recorded activity from macaque inferotemporal and prefrontal cortex during binocular flash suppression. Within a stable visual percept, population activity alternated between representations of the consciously reported face and a suppressed face.

~59–64 msMedian dwell durations for the two inferred states; not an established human refresh rate.
